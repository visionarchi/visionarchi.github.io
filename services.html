What we do

Services

We build and prototype edge AI systems: vision pipelines, sensor data, and the hardware they run on. Here is where we can help.

Computer vision

Detection, tracking and image analysis, built to run on the device rather than in the cloud.

Visual perception

Camera pipelines and perception models that turn raw frames into usable signals for a product or a robot.

Sensor fusion

Combining camera, inertial and other sensor data into one reliable picture of what is happening.

MCU and FPGA work

Bringing AI and vision workloads down to microcontrollers and FPGAs where power and latency matter.

Prototyping

Fast proof-of-concept builds that show whether an idea works before it becomes a full project.

Learning content

Tutorials and build videos on our YouTube channel, for engineers and students who want to build the same things.
